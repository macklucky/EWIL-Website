About Our Club

Empowering the next generation of women in law.

Empowering Women in Law (EWIL) is an undergraduate organization dedicated to creating a space for students of all identities who are passionate about advancing women in the legal field.

What we offer

Opportunities to connect, learn, and grow.

Our organization creates a platform for undergraduate women interested in exploring legal careers to connect and access professional development, networking, legal writing opportunities, speaker events, and social events.

What members gain

More than a pre-professional organization.

Members can build connections, gain exposure to different legal careers, develop legal skills, gain writing experience, find career direction, and build meaningful friendships.

Our approach

EWIL fosters a space where women’s voices are heard, legitimized, and put to use.

Unlike many pre-professional organizations at Cornell, EWIL has an all-inclusive membership policy. We are built on creating an inclusive space for anyone having a shared interest in furthering Women in Law.
