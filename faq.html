FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When are the meetings? Where do you meet?

We host bi-weekly meetings throughout the semester held on Central Campus.

Are there applications? What is the interview process like?

Unlike many pre-professional organizations at Cornell, EWIL has an all-inclusive membership policy. We are built on creating an inclusive space for anyone having a shared interest in furthering Women in Law. Even though we do not have a formal application process, feel free to reach out to an E-Board member to learn more about the club and how to get involved.

What types of law are members interested in?

One of the great things about EWIL is that members are interested in a wide range of legal fields. Throughout the year EWIL hosts guest speakers with a variety of experiences in the legal field such as corporate law, public interest, judges, risk management professionals, and more.
